## Why we build it this way

A voice assistant hears everything in the room it sits in. Most of them send that somewhere else to be understood, and the company on the other end decides how long the device keeps working and what it is allowed to do.

We think that is the wrong arrangement. The slogan is the whole argument: your home, your voice, your AI. A Satellite1 runs the wake word and the audio processing on the device, talks to a Home Assistant instance you control, and needs no internet connection to answer you.

Hardware outlives the companies that make it, so we design for the day we are not here. The boards are modular, the firmware is open, the enclosure models are published, and the documentation is detailed enough to rebuild from. If we disappeared tomorrow, you could keep yours running.

<div class="fph-principles" markdown>
<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Trust through verification__

    ---

    Our products must be auditable by anyone, so that the privacy claim is something you can check rather than something you take on faith.

-   __User continuity__

    ---

    If the company closes, a product is discontinued, or we change something you do not want, you should still be able to maintain and rebuild what you own.

-   __Right to modify__

    ---

    You can modify the products and build derivative works, and redistribute those changes on the same open terms. We just ask that you contribute back where you can.

-   __Technology neutral__

    ---

    The hardware should work across a wide range of platforms and protocols rather than locking you into one ecosystem, ours included.

</div>
</div>

Documentation is one of those principles rather than an afterthought. Every piece of work is supposed to ship with enough detail for someone else to replicate it, modify it and contribute to it, which is what this site is for.
