The **Enclosure Kit** is the printed two-way speaker cabinet for the Satellite1, supplied as finished parts so you don't have to print it yourself. It has built-in pockets for the LD2450 mmWave sensor and the external WiFi antenna.

## What's in the Box

- **Front speaker chamber** with a pocket for the LD2450 mmWave sensor
- **Back speaker chamber** with bass port and a dedicated pocket for the external antenna
- **Multi-material top plate** and transparent LED diffuser
- **Screw guide tool** (PETG, 15% infill)
- **Extra-long ball-head hex wrench**
- **Sealing foam**
- **22 × M3×10mm mounting screws**

Parts are printed in Bambu Lab PETG-HF — Dark Gray (33103) or White (33100).

## What's Not Included

!!! warning "This kit is the cabinet only"

    Speaker drivers, crossover, audio cables, and the Satellite1 boards are all sold separately.

## Printing It Yourself

You don't have to buy the parts — the 3MF project files, STL, and STEP geometry are published for anyone who wants to print or remix the enclosure at home.

- [FutureProofHomes GitHub](https://github.com/FutureProofHomes/Satellite1-Enclosures/tree/main/OEM%20Enclosures/Satellite1.1%20Smart%20Speaker%20Enclosure%20Kit)
- [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/models/2615819-futureproofhomes-oem-satellite1-1-smart-speaker)

!!! info "Recommended print settings"

    - 0.4mm nozzle with dry, Bambu-grade PETG
    - 3 wall perimeters and 30% cubic infill for the front and back speaker chambers
    - 3 wall perimeters and 15% cubic infill for the top plate, screw guide, and diffuser
    - Print the diffuser in transparent PETG or white PLA so the LEDs read clearly
    - No supports required
