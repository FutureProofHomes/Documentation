The **20W Speaker Electronics Kit** supplies the audio components that go inside a Satellite1 speaker enclosure — drivers, crossover, and every cable needed to wire them to the Hat board's amplifier.

If you already have a Dev Kit and are printing our enclosures at home, this is the kit that turns it into a two-way speaker.

## What's in the Box

### 3" Mid-Range Woofer — 15W, 20W peak @ 4Ω

Clear, powerful mid-range with strong bass response.

- 80 Hz – 6000 Hz frequency response
- 78mm round frame
- 86 dB sensitivity

### 1.2" Neodymium Dome Tweeter — 15W, 20W peak @ 4Ω

Smooth, detailed high-frequency response.

- 2.5 kHz – 20 kHz frequency response
- Aluminum heat sink for stable performance

### 2-Way Passive Crossover

Distributes high and low frequencies to the correct driver, with optional bass and treble boost.

### Cables and Hardware

- 2 × red 5.3mm speaker cables
- 2 × black 2.8mm speaker cables
- 1 × JST-XH cable (connects to the Hat's amplified speaker output)
- Mounting screws, hex wrench, and airtight sealing strip

## What You Also Need

This kit is audio components only. Pair it with:

- The [Dev Kit](satellite1-dev-kit.md) — the boards that drive the speaker
- The [Enclosure Kit](satellite1-enclosure-kit.md), or an enclosure you printed yourself

!!! info "Using your own drivers instead"

    The enclosure is acoustically and mechanically engineered around these drivers. Substituting alternatives, or modifying the enclosure to fit them, may affect both sound quality and wake-word performance.

Individual components are also sold separately if you only need to replace one part.
